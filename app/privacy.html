Privacy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

TipNet Teams is built so your numbers stay with you. In short: everything you type lives on your own device, and there is no TipNet Teams account, no analytics and no tracking.

This page is for two kinds of people. A venue is a business (a bar, a restaurant, a hotel) whose manager buys TipNet Teams. Staff are the people who work there and use the app for their own pay.

What is stored, and where

Your paystub numbers, nights, pay periods, budget entries and settings are stored in your browser on this device (in IndexedDB, with a backup copy in local storage). They are never uploaded to a TipNet Teams server, because TipNet Teams does not have one.

Staff pay stays private

A manager never sees what a staff member earns, keeps or spends. Joining a venue in the app does not share your pay, your nights or your budget with the venue or with us. The venue only receives the count described under "Staff Perks Credit" below.

Venue codes and tip sheets

A manager sets up a venue code, a QR poster, a tip-pool split and printable tip sheets. These hold only what the manager typed. A venue code carries the venue name, the pay schedule and payday, the jobs and their rates, the tip-outs, and a random venue id; no one's pay, hours or name. A tip sheet carries the venue name and id, the night, and the names, hours and tip amounts of the split. They are worked out and kept on the manager's own device and are not sent to us. A code travels inside its link, after the #, which browsers do not send to any server. If a manager prints or shares a code or a sheet, it goes where they choose.

Staff Perks Credit

If you join a venue in the app and then subscribe to TipNet Budget, the checkout link carries your venue's random id, so the venue can be credited for your subscription. The code goes to Lemon Squeezy with your order. We use it only to count active Budget subscriptions for each venue. The venue sees a count, never names. Subscribing is always your own choice. See the Terms for how the credit works.

Our website host

TipNet Teams' files are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub receives visitors' IP addresses and basic request information when the app's files are downloaded. See GitHub's privacy statement. TipNet Teams itself adds no tracking on top of that. If our website uses a custom domain name, that domain is managed by Cloudflare, which may also forward emails sent to our contact address to our inbox. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What never leaves your device

Your earnings, tips, paystub details, nights, budget and backup codes are never sent anywhere by TipNet Teams. If you use Save to iCloud or Google Drive, the backup file goes where you pick in your phone’s share menu, under that service’s terms.

The one network connection

The only time TipNet Teams talks to the internet is to check a license key with Lemon Squeezy, and only if you use a paid product (TipNet Teams for venues, or the TipNet Budget add-on for staff). That check sends your license key and a random device name that the app made up. It does not send your name, your email address or anything you entered in the app.

Payments

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record for every purchase. It handles your payment details, receipts and tax under its own privacy policy: lemonsqueezy.com/privacy. TipNet Teams never sees your card number.

No analytics, cookies or tracking

TipNet Teams uses no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no tracking of any kind. Your browser may keep a small copy of the app so it works offline. It also remembers your light or dark choice.

Backups are plain text

A backup code is your data written as plain text. Anyone who has the code can read it, so keep it private, and do not post it anywhere public. A backup never includes your license keys.

Deleting everything

Open Setup and tap Erase everything to remove your nights and settings from this device. Uninstalling the app or clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything too. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing else for us to delete. Your payment records stay with Lemon Squeezy.
