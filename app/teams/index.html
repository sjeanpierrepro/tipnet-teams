TipNet Teams

Your tip software shows what staff earned. TipNet Teams shows what they keep.

TipNet Teams is the manager edition of TipNet, the free app your bartenders and servers use to estimate their take-home pay after tax, one night at a time.

What you get as a manager

  • Venue setup QR. Print one poster. Staff scan it and join your venue.
  • Tip-pool calculator. Split a night's tips by hours or points, in seconds.
  • Tip sheets. Print or share a clear sheet of how a night was split.

These tools unlock with TipNet Teams. Everything you type stays on your own device.

What your staff get, free

  • Their tips and take-home pay estimate for every night, with their own tax numbers.
  • Pay periods, tip-outs and a backup of their own data.

Their numbers are private. You never see what any staff member earns or keeps, and joining your venue shares none of it.

Budget for staff, and the Staff Perks Credit

TipNet Budget is an optional add-on for staff: $1.99 a month or $20 a year. It plans their bills, spending and savings around their take-home.

When a staff member at your venue keeps a Budget subscription, your venue earns a credit worth 25% of what that person pays. Once a month we give it to you as a partial refund to your card on your venue's most recent TipNet Teams payment; the refunds on one payment never add up to more than it. Budget is never required for the job and is never taken out of anyone's wages or tips.

Worked example

10 staff keep a Budget subscription at $1.99 a month. Each earns your venue $0.50 a month, so your venue gets $5.00 back that month, as a partial refund on its most recent TipNet Teams payment.

A yearly subscriber counts as the yearly price divided by 12, times 25%, each month. Only active subscriptions count, counted monthly, and only while your TipNet Teams subscription is active. The credit counts at most 50 staff subscribers per venue, so on a monthly plan it is always less than your bill. You see a count, never names.

Questions

Can I see what my staff earn?

No. TipNet Teams has no staff pay data to show. Staff numbers stay on their own phones, and joining your venue does not share them. Your venue code and tip sheets hold only what you typed, and they are worked out on your own device.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from Lemon Squeezy's customer portal (the link above, or the link in your receipt email). It keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.

Do you give refunds?

No. You can cancel any time, but we do not refund a month or year you have already paid for. The one exception is the Staff Perks Credit, which is paid as a partial refund (below).

How many devices can use one key?

Up to 3, for example your phone, a tablet and a shared computer. To move a key, open Setup on the old device and tap "Remove from this device".

How is the Staff Perks Credit applied?

Once a month we count the active Budget subscriptions for your venue (at most 50), work out 25% of what each pays, and refund that amount to your card as a partial refund on your venue's most recent TipNet Teams payment. The refunds on one payment never add up to more than it (on a yearly plan, once they reach the yearly payment, they stop until the next one). We tell you the count, not who they are.

Do staff have to buy Budget?

Never. It is their choice. You cannot require it for the job, and it can never be deducted from wages or tips. Staff are told at checkout that their venue gets a small credit.
