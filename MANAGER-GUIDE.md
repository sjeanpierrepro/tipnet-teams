# TipNet Teams: manager guide

This is for the person who runs the venue (owner, GM, bar or floor manager). Staff don't need any of it. They open a link you give them and TipNet sets itself up.

The **Teams** tab (just before Setup) shows up once TipNet Teams is unlocked on your phone. A plan covers up to 50 staff per location; for a bigger venue, email hello@tipmynet.com for a larger plan (price on request). Everything you type there stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server, and the links and QR codes you make carry their information in the link itself.

## 1. Make your venue code

1. Open **Teams**, then **Venue code**.
2. Type your venue's name.
3. Pick how staff get paid (every week, every two weeks, twice a month...).
4. Pick the day the **current pay period started** and the day it **ends**, and the **payday** for that pay period (0 to 21 days after it ends).
5. Add your **jobs** and their rate, per hour or per shift. The first job is the main job.
6. Add the people staff **tip out** (barback, runner, host...): a percent of tips or a flat amount a night, from cash or through payroll. Leave this empty if there are none.

As soon as the form is complete you get your code: a **link** (with **Copy** and, on most phones, **Share**) and a **QR code**. If something is missing, TipNet says what in plain words.

What's in the code: the venue name, pay schedule, payday, jobs with rates, and tip-outs. What is not: anyone's gross pay, deductions, hours or name.

Your venue gets a private ID the first time you make a code. It stays on your phone, so when you change a rate and make the code again it is still the same venue. Staff who already joined can update from the new code instead of setting up twice.

You can also tap **Join this venue on this device** to set up your own TipNet with it.

## 2. Print the poster

Under your code, tap **Print the poster**. It prints the venue name, a big QR code, "Scan to set up TipNet Teams at (your venue)" and three short steps. Put it in the back of house or by the time clock. Use your phone's print or "Save as PDF" if you don't have a printer connected.

## 3. Run a tip pool

1. Open **Teams**, then **Tip pool**.
2. Type your venue name (it goes on the sheet) and pick the **night**.
3. Type the **pooled credit card tips** and **pooled cash tips**. Either can be 0.
4. Add everyone who worked: **name** (24 characters at most), **role**, and **hours** (8, 7.5, 7:30 or 45 min all work). Names, roles and points are remembered for next time. Hours are not: you type them each night.
5. Pick how to split:
   - **By hours**: each person's share of the hours worked.
   - **By points × hours**: hours times points, for example 2 points for a bartender and 1 for a runner.
   - **Equal**: everyone who worked gets the same.
   - **By role %**: you give each role a share of the pool (they have to add up to 100%), then it is split by hours inside the role.

The **tip sheet** appears as you type. Credit card tips and cash tips are split separately, in whole cents, so each column adds up to exactly what you pooled. If a cent is left over it goes to the person with the biggest fraction, and a tie goes to whoever is higher in your list. Someone with 0 hours gets 0. TipNet tells you plainly if nothing can be split (everyone has 0 hours, or the role shares are not 100%).

Tap **Print the tip sheet** to put it on paper.

## 4. Share a sheet

Under the tip sheet, tap **Share as a link or QR**. TipNet shows **"Anyone with this link or QR sees everyone's share."** before the link, because the sheet has every name and amount in it. Only share it with your team.

Staff open the link, pick their name, and TipNet opens **Tonight** for that date with their credit card tips, cash tips and hours already filled in and tip-outs unticked (the pool was already split). They look it over and tap **Save night**. Nothing is saved for them until they do.

A sheet holds up to 50 people. If you try to add more, TipNet says so: make a second sheet. A very full sheet (long names, many people) can also be too big to share as one link; TipNet says so: **split the staff into two sheets** and share each one. Printing still works. If it fits a link but not a QR code, you get the link only, with a note.

## The Staff Perks Credit

When a staff member who joined your venue keeps a TipNet Budget subscription, your venue earns 25% of what they pay. Once a month we give it to you as a partial refund to your card on your most recent TipNet Teams payment; the refunds on one payment never add up to more than that payment. We count at most 50 staff subscribers per venue, so on a monthly plan it is always less than your bill. You see a count, never names. Budget is never required for the job and can never be taken out of wages or tips.

## Good to know

- TipNet gives estimates, not tax or payroll advice. The tip sheet only does the arithmetic of your pool. Check it against your own policy and your local rules on tip pooling.
- Staff set up with your code, but they still type their own paystub numbers (gross pay, deductions, shifts). Their take-home is theirs alone.
- If you change the pay schedule, jobs or tip-outs, make the code again and share the new link. Staff update their jobs and tip-outs from it. Pay periods that have already ended keep the pay they were saved with.
